Changed `parse_date()` in `feed.py` (used by `parse_row()`) to accept both date styles: it strips the text, picks `%d/%m/%Y` when the value contains "/" and `%Y-%m-%d` otherwise, and still raises ValueError for anything else. Two named format constants (`OLD_FORMAT`, `NEW_FORMAT`) document the vendor switch. Files changed: feed.py only (test_feed.py is protected and could not be extended with new-style cases). The fixed check (the existing 3-test suite) passed, but it also passed before my change, so it does not prove the new behaviour; I confirmed that separately by running feed.py directly: "04/03/2026,10.5" and " 04/03/2026 " parse to 2026-03-04, old-style rows still work, and "32/13/2026,1", "banana,1" and "04-03-2026,1" raise ValueError. Risk: the format is chosen by the presence of "/", so any other separator the vendor might use (e.g. "04.03.2026" or "04-03-2026") is rejected, and a day-first date whose day <= 12 would be ambiguous if a separator-less or dash-separated variant ever appears.
