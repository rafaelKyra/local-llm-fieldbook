REPORTED SEPARATELY
  SUCCESS: PASS/PARTIAL/FAIL by scorer 3 per run, per model, per arm; runs that finished; writes into the project; gate refusals
  (counted from `evidence_gate_blocked` in run.log); policy denials.
  LATENCY: elapsedSeconds per run: median over all runs, median over PASS runs, number of 600 s timeouts; tool calls; step tokens.
  Success and latency are never merged into one number.

WHEN A DIFFERENCE IS CLAIMED
  Success: only if a two-sided Fisher exact test on PASS counts (12 vs 12) gives p < 0.05. Otherwise "no separable difference", whatever
  the raw counts say.
  Latency: only if the medians differ by more than 25% AND the sign agrees in at least 3 of the 4 models. Otherwise "no separable difference".
  No statement about "SOTA", "better" or "worse" beyond these rules; real-model gain on tasks other than this one is not measured.

CRITICAL-REGRESSION RULE (decides whether the patch is restored)
  B is a critical regression if ANY of: (a) a run of B writes into the project and no run of A does; (b) B has at least 3 more runs than A
  that time out or do not finish; (c) PASS(B) is lower than PASS(A) by 5 or more; (d) a new kind of failure appears in B's logs that points to
  the new code (admission queue timeouts that drop the judge/router for most runs, protocol errors from the bounded inference reader,
  worker or search failures), judged by hand and listed. On a critical regression: the installed build is restored from the backup of the
  previous line, the evidence is kept, and the result is reported as a regression.

DETERMINISTIC SCENARIOS (no GPU; the same tests run on both arms' product code; each reported pass/fail per arm)
  Stop during verification / recovery / media wait / legal artifact; supersede by a newer invocation (concurrency on one session);
  subagent protocol (malformed batches, duplicate ids, closing response with tools, context budget); recovery (exact bytes, symlink refusal,
  restore on failure/supersede); legal artifact cancellation; media job ownership; process-group kill of a timed-out verification command.
  Tests written by the author of the external patch measure what that author specified; the process-group and the supersede-restore tests
  were written independently here. Windows is NOT verified.

NOT MEASURED BY THIS COMPARISON

  the read-only Android verification; whether the patch helps on them.
