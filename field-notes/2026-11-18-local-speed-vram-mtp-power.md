Date: 2026-11-18 (observations from 2026-11-17 and 2026-11-18)
Model(s) and build: a 27B dense Qwen3.8 build (Q4_K_S, a re-quantised "MTP" repackage), loaded in LM Studio with a 75,008-token context; later Qwen3.8-Flash-Next GSQ-RCO IQ3_S served by Strata.
Setup (runtime, quant, context, settings): one RTX 3090 24 GB, dual Xeon, llama.cpp backend inside LM Studio, then Strata (llama.cpp-based, MTP drafting, 256K context).
The work: overnight screening runs that suddenly became three to ten times slower than expected; four separate causes found in two days.
What happened:
1. **6 tok/s instead of about 20: the model was split between GPU and CPU.** The server log showed \`--n-gpu-layers 45\` of 65. The cause was not the model: LM Studio's *embedding model* (a 0.6B model that the application's skill search loads) held 6.4 GB of the 3090, so the 27B no longer fitted. Unloading the embedding model and reloading with \`lms load <model> --gpu max --context-length 75008 --parallel 1\` gave 63 layers on the GPU and 19 to 23 tok/s. This confirms, once, the earlier claim in this repository that LM Studio can silently split a model between GPU and CPU. To check: \`nvidia-smi --query-compute-apps=pid,used_memory --format=csv\` and the \`--n-gpu-layers\` argument of the running \`llama-server\`.
2. **20 tok/s instead of 45 to 48: the speculative-decoding head was gone.** Server logs up to the evening of 2026-11-17 show a draft being accepted about 95% of the time (mean draft length 3.0 to 3.9) at 45 to 48 tok/s; after the reload in step 1 there is no draft line at all and generation is 49.7 ms per token, 20 tok/s. The model on disk carried "MTP" in its name but \`lms load --speculative-draft-mtp\` failed with "requires a GGUF model with a bundled supported MTP head": the repackaged file had no head. The files that had run with MTP (other quantisations of the same family) were no longer on disk. A model name that says MTP is not evidence of an MTP head; the loader's answer is.
3. **A power limit.** The card ran at 223 W of a 250 W limit (default 390 W) and 1,680 of 2,130 MHz. Not changed (it needs root); noted because it caps every figure here.
4. **Strata with MTP drafting: 67 to 84 tok/s** on the first two 400-token probes (draft acceptance 74 to 80%), the GPU full (22.4 GB held), so nothing else can be loaded beside it. End to end, though, a kit run on Strata took about 30 to 90 s with 1,250 to 4,900 output tokens, because it thinks much longer than the other models: a high decode speed does not mean short runs.
What I did not check: tokens per second under a long context, the effect of the power limit, or any figure on another machine. No repeat of any of the speeds above beyond the two probes and the log lines quoted.
